# ADR template — storygroove-be

Copy this file to `architecture/ADRs/NNNN-short-slug.md` (next number in sequence) and replace section bodies with real content.

## Title

Short imperative title of the decision.

## Status

One of: Proposed, Accepted, Deprecated, Superseded by ADR-XXXX.

## Context

What problem, constraint, or change forced a decision? Include relevant history, scale, and stakeholders.

## Decision

State the chosen approach in one or two tight paragraphs.

## Consequences

**Positive**

- Benefit or simplification.

**Negative**

- Cost, rigidity, or operational burden — note mitigations.

**Neutral**

- Follow-up work or documentation debt.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Option A** — why rejected.
2. **Option B** — why rejected.
